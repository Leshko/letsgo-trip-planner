# Getting 12 Android testers from a social post

Google's rule for new personal developer accounts: before the Android app can
go public, **at least 12 testers must be opted in to a closed test for 14 days
in a row**. A post on your socials works well. Testers just need a Google
account and any Android phone; they must be real, different people (no
emulators or made-up accounts, which can get the developer account banned).

Aim for **15–20 testers**, so the count stays at 12+ if a few drop out.

---

## 1. One-time setup (about 10 minutes, after your Play account is approved)

**A. A Google Group anyone can join** (this is the tester list)
1. Go to https://groups.google.com → **Create group**.
2. Name: `LetsGo Together testers`. Group email: e.g. `letsgo-testers@googlegroups.com`.
3. Privacy settings: **Who can search for group:** Anyone on the web. **Who can join group:** Anyone can join. **Who can view conversations / post:** Group members (or Group managers) only.
4. Create. Copy the group's web link (https://groups.google.com/g/letsgo-testers).

**B. The closed test in Play Console**
1. Play Console → LetsGo Together → **Test and release → Testing → Closed testing** → **Create track** (or use the default one).
2. **Testers** tab → **Google Groups** → add `letsgo-testers@googlegroups.com` → Save.
3. I upload the first build to this track for you (the release builds come once your accounts exist). Once it's live, the Testers tab shows the **opt-in link** ("Join on the web"). Copy it.

Now you have two links for the post: **the group link** and **the opt-in link**.

## 2. The post (copy, paste, fill in the two links)

**Facebook / Instagram / X**
> 🚗 Favor to ask! I built an app for planning family trips together: **LetsGo Together**. Everyone votes on things to do, builds the plan, shares photos, and on the trip you can see each other on a family map.
>
> Google needs 12+ people to test it on Android for 2 weeks before it can go in the Play Store. If you have an Android phone, it takes 2 minutes:
> 1️⃣ Join the tester group: [GROUP LINK]
> 2️⃣ Tap "Become a tester": [OPT-IN LINK]
> 3️⃣ Install it from the Play Store link on that page
> 4️⃣ Keep it installed for 2 weeks and open it a few times (join my demo trip with code [DEMO CODE] and vote on something!)
>
> iPhone friends: it's coming to the App Store too 🙏 Thank you!!

**Text message (family / friends)**
> Hey! Could you help me test my trip app on your Android phone? 2 minutes: join [GROUP LINK], then tap "Become a tester" at [OPT-IN LINK] and install it. Keep it for 2 weeks and open it now and then (demo trip code [DEMO CODE]). Thank you!

Make a **demo trip** first (on the website, dates over the next two weeks)
and put its invite code in the post, so testers have something real to tap
around in.

## 3. Watching progress
- Play Console → **Closed testing** → your track shows how many testers have **opted in**. The 14 days count from when you have 12 opted in, and the count must stay at 12+ the whole time.
- Halfway through, post a quick reminder ("thanks testers! if you haven't opened LetsGo in a while, take a peek 🙏"). Google looks at whether testers actually used the app, not just installed it.
- Collect feedback: ask testers to reply in the group or text you. Write down what they said; you'll need it below.

## 4. Applying for production (after 14 days)
Play Console → **Dashboard → Apply for production**. Google asks these
questions; suggested answers (edit with what really happened):

- **How did you recruit testers?** Friends and family through a social media post and text messages, using a Google Group as the tester list.
- **How easy was it to recruit testers?** Fairly easy; family members who plan trips together were happy to help.
- **Describe the engagement from testers.** Testers joined a demo trip, voted on ideas, added plan items, posted on the trip wall, and tried notifications and the family map. (Add numbers if you have them, e.g. "15 testers, most opened it several times a week".)
- **Summarize the feedback and how you collected it.** Collected through the tester group and messages. (List 2–3 real things people said.)
- **Who is the intended audience?** Families and friend groups planning a trip together (reunions, vacations, birthdays).
- **How does your app provide value?** One place for the whole group to suggest and vote on activities and restaurants, build a day-by-day plan, share photos, and find each other on the trip, without endless group texts.
- **Expected installs in the first year?** A realistic small number (e.g. 1,000–10,000).
- **What changed based on testing?** List any fixes made during the test (I'll keep a list for you).
- **How did you decide it's ready?** Testers completed the main flows (join, vote, plan, wall, photos, notifications, family map) without blocking issues during the 14-day test.

Approval usually takes a few days. After that, the app can go public in the Play Store.
