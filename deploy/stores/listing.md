## Description (both stores)
LetsGo Together is where the whole family plans the trip, together.

No more 40-message group texts. Share one invite link and everyone joins in seconds: no passwords, just their name.

PLAN TOGETHER
• Pitch things to do and places to eat. Paste a link and LetsGo pulls in the photos, address and menu.
• Vote with one tap. The family favorites float to the top.
• Build the day-by-day plan and drag things between days.
• Keep the house details in one place: address, check-in, door code, who sleeps where.

ON THE TRIP
• Trip mode switches on by itself on day one: today's plan, directions, and the latest from the family up front.
• Family map: anyone who wants to can share where they are, so nobody gets lost at the theme park. It's opt-in, only during the trip, and switches off by itself at midnight after the last day.
• The wall for "running late", "who's bringing the cooler", and everything in between.
• One shared photo album. Everyone adds their photos; anyone can download them all.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Only the people in your trip see your trip.
• Location sharing is off until you turn it on, and your location is deleted when the trip ends.
• No ads. Nothing sold.
